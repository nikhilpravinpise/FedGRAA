## Brief description

FedPGO is a personalized federated learning method for IoT intrusion detection. It separates client gradient updates into components aligned with and orthogonal to a global gradient, then combines them with a personalization-aware fusion strategy. The article reports experiments on CICIoT2023 and CIC-IDS-2017.

This paper is related work for the FedGRAA-v2 evaluation. The repository's analysis and implementation decisions are documented in [Final_Stage_C.md](../Final_Stage_C.md) and [STAGE_C_FEDPGO_FAITHFUL_PLAN_2026-07-31.md](../STAGE_C_FEDPGO_FAITHFUL_PLAN_2026-07-31.md).
